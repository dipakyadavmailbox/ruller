# NOTE: the legal and contact pages are deliberately NOT disallowed.
#
# They were previously blocked here to save crawl budget, which broke two
# things at once:
#   1. AdSense review requires the privacy policy, terms, and a contact
#      route to be reachable by its crawler. Blocking them is a common
#      cause of "site does not comply with policies" rejections.
#   2. A page that is Disallowed can never have its noindex tag read,
#      because the crawler is not allowed to fetch the page to see it.
#      Blocking and noindexing the same URL is self-defeating.
#
# The pages that should stay out of the index carry a robots meta tag
# instead, which is the mechanism that actually works.

Disallow: /404
